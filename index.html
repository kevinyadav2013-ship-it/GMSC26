Competition Structure

Round 1

Participants take the GMSC Round 1 examination during the official examination period.

Participants who obtain full marks in Round 1 qualify for Round 2.

Round 2

Participants who achieve full marks in Round 1 are eligible for the second round.

🏆 Full-Marks Award

A participant who obtains full marks in both Round 1 and Round 2 will receive a $250 award, subject to the official GMSC rules.
