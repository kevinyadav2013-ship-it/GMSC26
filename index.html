Competition Features

Designed to provide a professional online examination experience.

⏱️

Timed Examination

Participants receive a fixed examination time with automatic submission when the timer ends.

🛡️

Proctored Mode

Camera, microphone and browser controls can be used to create a more secure examination environment.

📊

Results & Ranking

Participants can receive their score and, when enabled by the organizers, ranking information.

Important Rules

  • Participants must follow all official GMSC instructions.
  • The examination must be completed within the allotted time.
  • Participants may be required to provide camera and microphone permissions for proctored examinations.
  • Participants must not use unauthorized assistance during the test.
  • Any suspicious examination events may be recorded for review.
